The original titles and individual download URLs are recorded in [sources.json](sources.json), together with the original and adapted SHA-256 hashes. These illustrations retain their CC BY 3.0 license; the extension's MIT license does not replace it. The creator does not endorse this extension.

Adaptations: replaced `currentColor` with the fixed grey-blue ink `#7b8794` for standalone image rendering, then validated and normalized the SVG with Bottle's static SVG allowlist. Geometry is preserved. Chinese catalog labels and semantic tags were added for discovery. No scripts, external resources or new SVG features were enabled.

The pre-existing `wave-line`, `corner-flower` and `star-border` illustrations are not from SVG Doodles and are not included in this third-party attribution list.

When reusing these imported illustrations, retain the creator credit, source and license links, original titles, and a note of adaptations. See the linked license for its full terms.
